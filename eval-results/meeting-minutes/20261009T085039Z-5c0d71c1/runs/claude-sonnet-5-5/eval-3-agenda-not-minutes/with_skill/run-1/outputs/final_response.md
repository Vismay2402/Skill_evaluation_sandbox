I've drafted the 45-minute agenda and saved it to `outputs/agenda.md`. I didn't use the meeting-minutes skill, since it covers minutes rather than agendas.

| Time | Topic |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | Kick-off and review of last sync's actions |
| 0:05–0:20 | Cost review (15 min): spend vs. budget, main drivers, quick wins. Decision on which optimisations to pursue. |
| 0:20–0:33 | dbt upgrade (13 min): version gap, breaking changes, rollout and rollback plan, effort. Go/no-go and target date. |
| 0:33–0:42 | Power BI request (9 min): business need, whether the data already exists, cost and licence impact. Accept, decline or defer. |
| 0:42–0:45 | Wrap-up: decisions, owners and due dates read back |

The file also has pre-read suggestions for each item. The date, attendees, topic leads and facilitator are placeholders for you to fill in. I put the most time on the cost review because it's the broadest topic. Move minutes around if the Power BI request is more urgent.
