## 1. Cost review (15 min)
- Spend vs. budget: month-to-date and trend (warehouse compute, storage, orchestration, BI licensing)
- Top cost drivers and any anomalies or spikes
- Optimization opportunities (query/model tuning, warehouse sizing, scheduling, retention)
- **Decision:** which savings actions to commit to, with owners and dates

## 2. dbt upgrade (15 min)
- Current vs. target version; key breaking changes and deprecations
- Readiness: test coverage, package compatibility, CI status, adapter support
- Rollout plan: dev/staging validation, cutover window, rollback approach
- Risks and effort estimate
- **Decision:** go/no-go, timeline, and owner

## 3. Power BI request (9 min)
- Request summary: who is asking, the business need, and the desired timeline
- Data requirements: sources, existing models vs. new work, refresh cadence
- Impact: effort, licensing/capacity cost, and governance or security considerations
- **Decision:** accept, defer, or decline; if accepted, assign an owner and a first step

## Pre-reads (please review beforehand)

- dbt release notes and the upgrade checklist
- Power BI request ticket or brief
